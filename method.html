A Monday · reconstruction

A landing page for a client.

Not a named client. A faithful reconstruction of the job: a page that has to get a yes this week. Steal it. Then replace the facts with yours.

See

The decision, before the model.

The client sells a six-week positioning sprint to founders who already have a product and a homepage that does not convert. They do not need “a modern site.” They need this week’s yes: Book the $400 diagnostic.

Good looks like: one reader (the founder), one offer (the diagnostic), one action (a calendar link), a headline that can be false. If the headline cannot be argued with, it is decoration.

Structure

Give the model a job. Then the prompt.

Role: direct-response, not brand poet. Length: hero, problem, offer, what happens, close. Kill-conditions: no “we help companies,” no second offer, no “book a free call,” no stock metrics, no carousel of everything they also do.

The prompt is the last sentence. Evaluation is written first: if a stranger cannot say who it is for and what they do next, it dies.

Stress

The first pass. What dies.

Unlock your brand’s true potential. We partner with ambitious companies to deliver world-class positioning, strategy, and growth. Book a free call — or explore our services.
  • No who. “Ambitious companies” is everyone.
  • Two actions. Free call and a menu. That is a brochure.
  • Adjective pile. Potential, world-class, ambitious. None can be false.
  • No price. No next step a founder can do today.
