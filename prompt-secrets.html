Draft a landing hero and a for / not-for for [OFFER] sold to [WHO].

Write the evaluation list first, then the copy.

The copy must pass:
- A stranger can say who, what, and the price in five seconds.
- The headline can be argued with.
- One action — the paid one.
- No metric or proof that was not in this brief.
- If a competitor can paste their name in, rewrite.

Do not offer a free call. Do not invent clients.
