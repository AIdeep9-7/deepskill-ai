The DeepSkill Loop: a method for thinking with models.

See. Structure. Stress. Ship.

Unnamed methods evaporate. You remember a loop. You do not remember a thread.

See — frame the work before a model is involved. What decision is being made? What does good look like?

Structure — give the model a job. Role, constraints, context, how you will evaluate. The prompt is the last sentence, not the first.

Stress — interrogate the output. Adversarial checks. Taste. Domain truth. This is where amateurs stop and operators start.

Ship — turn it into a paid artifact. An offer, a brief, a decision, a deliverable. If it never leaves the chat window, it doesn’t count.

We put this on every product page so you always know which pass you’re in. The longer version lives on the method page. Seventy-five starting prompts: Prompt Secrets.
