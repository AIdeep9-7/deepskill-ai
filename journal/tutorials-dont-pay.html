Most AI education is a tutorial. That’s why it doesn’t pay.

A short argument for method over tools.

A tutorial teaches you which button to click. That was useful when the buttons were scarce. They aren’t. Every model ships with a tour. The tour expires when the chrome moves.

What does not expire: knowing what “good” looks like before you open the chat. Knowing how to reject a fluent wrong answer. Knowing how to turn the remainder into something a client will pay for.

If your education can be replaced by a changelog, it was never education. It was onboarding.

Operators don’t need more onboarding. They need a method they can run next week, on whatever model is current.

That method, here, has a name. The DeepSkill Loop. If you want seventy-five starting prompts while you learn it, that’s Prompt Secrets — $4.
